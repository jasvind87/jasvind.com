A Singapore-focused Fund Designed for Dynamic Income Investors

As fixed income and cash strategies face rising rate pressure, one may want to consider diversifying closer to home. The United SG Dynamic Income Fund (The “Fund”) adopts a dynamic multi-asset approach—combining Singapore bonds, equities, and S-REITs into a balanced portfolio which currently distributes a ~4.0% p.a.* target monthly income.

*Targeted distributions are not guaranteed and are subject to the Manager's discretion. Distributions may be made out of income, capital gains, or capital, which may reduce the Net Asset Value (NAV) of the share class.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I invest in the United SG Dynamic Income Fund Class A DIST on UOBAM Invest?
    • Log in to the UOBAM Invest app and navigate to the Invest tab.
    • Select Fund Direct and complete the account setup process if you have not already done so.
    • Once your account has been set up, browse the available funds or use the search function to locate United SG Dynamic Income Fund.
    • Ensure that you select Class A SGD Dist before proceeding with your investment.
    • Click Buy Now to invest immediately or Add to Cart if you would like to complete the transaction at a later time.
    • Follow the payment instructions provided in the app to transfer your investment amount. Please ensure that the correct account reference is included in your funds transfer to avoid any delays in processing.

    The cut-off time for transactions on each Dealing Day is 3:00 p.m. Singapore Time. Orders and payments received after the cut-off time will be processed on the next Dealing Day.

  • Is the monthly distribution guaranteed?

    No. The Fund aims to make monthly distributions of up to 4% p.a., but the amount and frequency are not guaranteed. Distributions may be made from income, capital gains and/or capital.

  • What is the difference between Class A SGD Acc and Class A SGD Dist?

    Class A SGD Acc reinvests income into the Fund. Class A SGD Dist aims to pay monthly distributions to investors.

  • What is the cut-off time for submitting an order?

    Orders should be submitted before 3.00 p.m. on each Dealing Day. Orders submitted after the cut-off will be processed on the next Dealing Day.

  • How long will my investment take to be processed?

    Once your investment order has been submitted, the Fund's Net Asset Value (NAV) and allocated units are typically reflected by the next business day. You can monitor the status of your order at any time through the UOBAM Invest app.

  • How do I withdraw my investment, and when will I receive the proceeds?

    To redeem your investment, submit a withdrawal request through the UOBAM Invest app. The redemption proceeds will be credited to your UOBAM Invest Cash Account upon settlement, which is typically T+5 business days for the United SG Dynamic Income Fund.

    If you wish to transfer the proceeds to your bank account, simply initiate a withdrawal from your UOBAM Invest Cash Account once the funds have been credited.

  • When are distributions paid, and how will I receive them?

    Distributions for Class A SGD Dist are generally paid on a monthly basis and will be credited directly to your UOBAM Invest Cash Account. Once the distribution has been credited, you may transfer the proceeds to your bank account by submitting a withdrawal request through the UOBAM Invest app.

  • Are there any sales charges?

    No.

  • What fees apply to my investment?

    There are no additional fees. The only applicable fee is the Fund's management fee, which is reflected in the Fund's Net Asset Value (NAV).

  • Can I speak to someone before investing?

    As Fund Direct is a self-directed investment portfolio, UOBAM Invest is unable to provide investment advice or make recommendations. Should you have any operational or account-related queries regarding your investments, you can contact UOBAM Invest support via email UOBAMInvest@UOBGroup.com.

  • Where can I track the Fund’s price and performance?

    You can view your investment and its performance in the UOBAM Invest app. Fund information is also available on the UOBAM website⁠.

  • Can I set up a Regular Savings Plan for the Fund?

    Yes, the Fund is available under the Regular Savings Plan (RSP).

    To set up a recurring investment, log in to the UOBAM Invest app and navigate to the Fund Direct section. You can browse the available funds or use the search function to locate the United SG Dynamic Income Fund. Once you have selected the Fund, choose the Regular Savings Plan (RSP) option and follow the on-screen instructions to set your preferred investment amount and contribution frequency. Your investments will then be made automatically according to the schedule you have selected.
