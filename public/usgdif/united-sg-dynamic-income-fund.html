Understand what “up to 6% p.a.” means.

A distribution is not the same as an investment return.

The Fund aims to make regular monthly distributions of up to 6% p.a. for its Distribution Classes, but the distributions are not guaranteed and may be varied or suspended.

Distributions may be made out of income, capital gains and/or capital. Where distributions are made out of capital, this may amount to a return of part of your original investment and will reduce the Fund's net asset value.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does “up to 6% p.a. monthly income” mean?

    For Class A SGD Dist, the Fund aims to make regular monthly distributions of up to 6% p.a. The distribution is not guaranteed and UOBAM may vary the rate, frequency or amount. Distributions may be made out of income, capital gains and/or capital.

  • Is the 6% a guaranteed return?

    No. The distribution policy is not a guaranteed investment return. Your total return depends on both the distributions received and changes in the value of your units.

  • Can distributions be paid out of my capital?

    Yes. If distributions are made out of capital, this may amount to a return of part of your original investment and will reduce the net asset value of the relevant share class. It may also reduce future returns.

  • What is the difference between Class A SGD Acc and Class A SGD Dist?

    Class A SGD Dist is designed for investors who want the Fund's distribution feature. Class A SGD Acc is an accumulation share class, where returns remain invested in the Fund rather than being distributed as regular cash payouts.

  • What does the Fund invest in?

    The Fund invests across five asset classes: Singapore equities, Singapore REITs, Singapore bonds, Singapore money market instruments and Asia equities. It invests primarily in Singapore.

  • How does AI-Augmentation@UOBAM work in this Fund?

    UOBAM combines artificial intelligence and machine-learning analysis with human investment expertise. The technology works alongside the investment team to support the research and allocation process.

  • How often is the portfolio rebalanced?

    The Fund is rebalanced quarterly based on AI-based analyses, so the allocation across asset classes can change as market conditions and investment signals evolve.

  • What is the minimum investment?

    For Class A, the Fund's stated minimum subscription is S$1,000 initially and S$500 for subsequent investments. The amount displayed in UOBAM Invest will reflect the applicable requirements for the transaction.

  • What fees apply?

    The Class A management fee is currently 1.00% p.a., with a maximum of 2.50% p.a. A subscription fee of currently up to 3% (maximum 5%) applies under the Fund terms, although the actual charge may differ by distributor or purchase channel. Other platform fees or charges may also apply. Please refer to the Fund's prospectus and the applicable UOBAM Invest Fund Direct fee information shown before you transact.

  • How do I buy the Fund on UOBAM Invest?

    Log in to UOBAM Invest, select Fund Direct, search for United SG Dynamic Income Fund, review the fund information and documents, then select the share class and amount you wish to invest.
