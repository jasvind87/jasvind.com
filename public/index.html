01 Introduction

Growth, brand and the regulated craft of talking about money.

I lead marketing and communications for a Singapore asset manager — brand, performance, digital and content across institutional, intermediary and retail audiences.

Most of my work sits where three things meet: commercial growth, creative storytelling, and the compliance reality of a MAS-regulated industry. Fund launches and ETF listings. Campaigns that have to convert and pass review. Executive communications that need to sound like a person.

Before asset management I ran growth across nine APAC markets in e-commerce, food tech and brokerage — building performance teams from scratch, and learning that a good campaign is mostly arithmetic wearing nice clothes.
